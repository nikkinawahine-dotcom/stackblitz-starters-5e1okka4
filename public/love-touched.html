Love Touched

Press a button on the worn HUD. The toy page is the set of controls that opens in the browser.

Buttons

Link toy
Opens a QR code. Scan it with Lovense Remote. The HUD says the toy is linked. Pressing it again while a toy is already linked keeps that link.
Give HUD
Lists people within 10 meters. The person you pick gets the toy page in their browser.
Stop
Turns the toy off.
Disconnect
Turns the toy off and drops the link. The next time needs a new QR from Link toy.
Options
Opens Titler and Hide.
Titler
Turns the floating text on or off. Plus and minus move it from 0 to 30. The text shows the HUD name, whether Lovense is connected, the toy name, and the battery. While the toy is running, the text shifts from green toward red as the level rises.
Hide
Makes the HUD invisible. The button still works. Press it again to show the HUD.

Controls

Toy
The drop down. Pick one connected toy, or all of them. Changing it stops what is running and shows the sliders for that toy. Under the drop down, the page names the toy and the controls it has.
Vibration
A slider from 0 to 100. Moving it sends that level to the toy. It eases to the new level instead of jumping.
Other sliders
Shown when the toy has them. Each one is 0 to 100: Vibration 1, Vibration 2, Vibration 3, Rotation, Pump, Thrusting, Fingering, Suction, Depth, Oscillate, Stroke range, and Position. Moving one sends that level. The page scales 0–100 to the toy’s real max.
For seconds
The run lasts for the number in Seconds, from 1 to 60.
Stay on
The sliders hold where you put them until you move them or press Stop. A pattern stays on until you tap it again. The browser remembers this choice.
Seconds
How long For seconds runs. Patterns use this number too.
Vibrate for those seconds
Starts the sliders that are above 0. With more than one slider in use, the button reads Run for those seconds. In Stay on, it reads Hold this level or Hold these levels. If every slider is at 0, nothing starts.
Pulse
Plays Lovense’s Pulse pattern for the seconds above, or until you tap it again in Stay on.
Wave
Plays Lovense’s Wave pattern the same way.
Fireworks
Plays Lovense’s Fireworks pattern the same way.
Earthquake
Plays Lovense’s Earthquake pattern the same way.
My patterns
Patterns saved on this avatar. Tap one to play it. They are still there the next time the page opens. Up to 12.
Make a pattern
Opens the builder. Drag the bars. Each bar is one beat, off at the bottom and full at the top, up to 50 beats.
Try it
Plays the bars you drew. At least one bar has to be above off.
Save for this avatar
Saves that pattern under the name you typed, up to 24 characters. The name is required.
Stop
Sets every slider to 0 and turns the toy off, including a pattern that is playing.
Disconnect
Unlinks the phone. Use Link toy in Second Life for a new QR.
Battery
The line under the buttons shows the toy’s battery from the phone.
